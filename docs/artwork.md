## Hero prompt

Create a polished, gently humorous editorial illustration for a public research project website about integrated risks and tasks. Wide landscape 16:9, high resolution. A small English rural construction site up a SINGLE narrow farm lane, cream and sage green countryside, dark teal ink outlines, warm ochre construction machinery and brick-red accents, warm off-white paper texture. Clear oblique/isometric bird's-eye composition: foreground-left a concrete mixer lorry waits safely BEFORE a shared farm gateway, its driver looking bemused; five unhurried cows and a farmer are walking along the same lane through that gateway toward a small market visible in the far right background. Behind the gateway uphill is the construction site with shuttering for foundations and two workers waiting by the unpoured foundation; the access lane is visibly the only entrance. One particularly nonchalant cow looks sideways at the lorry. This is a logistical delay, no collision or danger, no distressed animals. Make the shared bottleneck unmistakable at a glance: cows occupy a clearly bounded stretch of the very same single lane the lorry needs, no alternative road or bypass. Charming detailed architectural field-sketch meets high quality illustrated magazine cover, restrained palette, clear shapes and readable spatial relationships. Do NOT add text, labels, titles, speech bubbles, numbers, equations, logos, pseudo-diagrams, arrows or watermarks. Keep scene airy, accessible and intelligent, not clip-art or photorealism.
